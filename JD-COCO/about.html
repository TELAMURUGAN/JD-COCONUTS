WHO WE ARE

Your Partner in Coconut Trading

Welcome to JD Coconuts, a business focused on coconut trading and coconut-based natural materials. We aim to connect buyers and sellers and support enquiries for different coconut product categories.

From coconuts and raw coconut husk to coir fibre and coco peat, we help customers discuss their product requirements, quantities and business needs.

Our focus is on building reliable business relationships and exploring long-term opportunities in the coconut products industry.
